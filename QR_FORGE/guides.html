QR & Image Guides

Choosing a QR type

Use a URL QR for websites, Wi-Fi for network credentials, vCard for contact details, UPI for payments, and GPS for a location. Keep the encoded content accurate and test the result with a phone before printing.

Image resizing

Resize based on the destination. Social platforms often use fixed pixel dimensions, while forms and official documents may specify exact dimensions or file-size limits.

Image compression

Start with the smallest target that still preserves the details you need. JPEG and WebP are generally better choices than PNG for photographic images.

PNG vs JPG vs WebP

PNG is useful for transparency and graphics, JPG is widely compatible for photographs, and WebP can provide efficient compression for modern websites.

Passport and ID photos

Official photo requirements differ by country, authority and application. Use this site's presets only as a resizing convenience and always check the exact requirements of the authority requesting the photo.
