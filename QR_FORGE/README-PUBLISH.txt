QR Forge Studio — PRODUCTION / ADSENSE READY

1. Replace:
   YOUR-DOMAIN.com
   YOUR-EMAIL@example.com
   YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID

2. Buy your domain and deploy this folder to Cloudflare Pages.
3. After the site is live, add the domain in Google AdSense.
4. Use the exact AdSense verification code Google gives you.
5. After approval, replace ads.txt placeholder with your real publisher ID.
6. Update sitemap.xml and robots.txt to the real domain.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not use a fake AdSense publisher ID.
- AdSense approval is controlled by Google; this package prepares the site but cannot guarantee approval.
- The QR generator currently uses QRCode.js from a CDN.
- Review legal/privacy wording for your actual business, country, analytics and consent setup before launch.
