Frequently Asked Questions

Which QR formats can I create?

The generator supports plain text, URLs, Wi-Fi, vCard, email, SMS, phone, GPS, calendar events, WhatsApp, UPI, Bitcoin and custom data.

Can I create a QR code from my live location?

Yes. Use the Live Location button and allow your browser to provide your current coordinates.

Can I compress an image to a specific size?

Yes. Choose a purpose or enter a target in KB or MB. The compressor tries to reach the requested maximum while keeping the image usable.

Does background removal work on every image?

The built-in local remover works best when the background is a connected area with a reasonably consistent color. Complex photographs may require a dedicated segmentation service.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to use the browser-based tools.
