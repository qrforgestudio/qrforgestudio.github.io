About QR Forge Studio

QR Forge Studio is a browser-based collection of practical QR-code and image tools. The goal is to make common tasks such as generating QR codes, resizing images, cropping, converting formats, compressing files and preparing transparent PNGs simple and accessible.

Privacy by design

Where a tool can work locally in your browser, the processing is performed locally. Some features may use third-party libraries or services, which are identified in this site's documentation and privacy policy.

Our goal

We aim to provide clear tools, useful guides and a straightforward experience without unnecessary steps.
